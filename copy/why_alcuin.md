## browser-title
Our approach to AI in schools | The Alcuin Project

## meta-description
Alcuin's approach to AI in schools: practical experience for teachers, independent thinking for students, and decisions grounded in the learning goal.

## nav-label
Our approach

## hero-h1
Start with what the work is for.

## hero-body
Using AI well in a school means deciding what to ask of it, what to check, and what people need to do for themselves.

## teachers-heading
Teachers need time to learn the tools.

## teachers-body
Teachers need practical experience using AI on lessons, materials, and planning. They also need to recognize weak explanations, inaccurate information, and work that looks finished before it is ready for students.

A workshop can begin that learning. Continued practice and coaching help teachers apply it to their own classes.

## students-heading
Students need to understand the work they submit.

## students-body
An assignment can produce a finished piece and build a skill at the same time. Before deciding how AI may help, identify the knowledge or reasoning students need to practice.

A student learning to make an argument needs to choose and defend claims. Reading the instructions aloud may help that student get started; supplying the argument would take over the skill being taught.

Students also need to learn how AI works, check its claims, protect personal information, and explain how they used it.

## mission-heading
What we're working toward

## mission-body
Schools where teachers can use AI with confidence and students develop the knowledge and judgment to use it responsibly.

## work-heading
Our work

## work-body
We train and coach educators, advise schools, build custom technology solutions, and develop the standards, curriculum subscriptions, and classroom materials for schools teaching AI literacy.

## values-heading
Our core values

## values-intro
Seven, sequenced from the handshake to the child.

## values
- **Integrity** — We do what we say, by when we say, at the standard we say — and when we miss, we say so first.
- **Privacy** — We protect the privacy of students and teachers absolutely, even when protecting it costs us the contract.
- **Mastery** — We pursue mastery the way we ask teachers to: practicing, measuring, and improving our own craft continuously.
- **Critical Thinking** — We develop critical thinkers: every solution must strengthen, never shortcut, the thinking of teachers and students.
- **Core Solutions** — We work by cause and effect, never by crisis — if it doesn't reach the core problem, we don't call it a solution.
- **Empathy** — We listen before we prescribe: every engagement starts with the lived reality of teachers and students.
- **Teacher Centric** — We build for the teacher first, because every child's learning flows through them.
