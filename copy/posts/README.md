# Posts

One file per post. Filename becomes the URL slug: `ai-policy-notes.md`
builds `blog-ai-policy-notes.md` -> `blog-ai-policy-notes.html`.

Slots:

    ## title       required. The post headline.
    ## date        required. YYYY-MM-DD. Sorts the index, newest first.
    ## summary     required. One or two sentences, shown on the index.
    ## body        required. The post. Markdown-lite: paragraphs,
                   '- ' lists, 'N. ' lists, '### ' subheads.

For a post about someone else's article, add:

    ## link-url     the article's URL
    ## link-source  the publication
    ## link-author  the byline (optional)

Those three turn the post into a link post: the index and the post page
both credit the source and link out to it.

## What may be reproduced here

A link post carries Joseph's own commentary and, at most, a short quoted
passage in quotation marks with attribution. It never reproduces the
article, its images, or a rewritten version of its structure. If a
reader would not need to click through, too much has been copied.
Images from another publication are never copied or hotlinked.
