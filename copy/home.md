## principle-heading
What should students do for themselves?

## principle-body
Start with what the assignment is meant to teach. If students are learning to build an argument, they need to choose and defend their claims. Help with reading the instructions can make that work accessible without doing the reasoning for them.

## outcomes-heading
What students need to learn

## outcomes-intro
Students need the knowledge and judgment to work with AI and to work independently. The Alcuin framework organizes that learning around three outcomes.

## outcome-1-heading
Subject knowledge

## outcome-1-body
Students need a foundation in the subjects they study. That knowledge helps them recognize an implausible answer and explain their own reasoning.

## outcome-2-heading
Practical AI skills

## outcome-2-body
Students learn to choose an appropriate tool, give it useful information, and evaluate what it produces.

## outcome-3-heading
Independent judgment

## outcome-3-body
Students learn to verify claims, account for their use of AI, and recognize when the work needs to remain their own.
