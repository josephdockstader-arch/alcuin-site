School-based AI workshops, coaching, and advisory support. Work on your own curriculum, with goals and a scope agreed with your school.

## hero-body
Teach your staff to configure AI tools, build reusable workflows, and create materials for students, including course websites. Teachers learn to evaluate what they produce for accuracy, instructional quality, and the thinking each lesson asks students to do.

## sequence-heading
Training tailored to your school

## sequence-intro
We begin with your curriculum leader or school leadership to agree on the goals, then plan the training and follow-up support around your staff's needs. Schools can book individual services or a continuing engagement.

## step-1-heading
Plan with school leadership

## step-1-body
We discuss your curriculum, your staff's experience with AI, and what you want the training to accomplish. Together, we choose the focus, format, and scope.

## step-2-heading
Build practical skills

## step-2-body
Teachers work with the tools and materials relevant to those goals. Sessions can cover tool configuration, reusable workflows, instructional content, and course websites, with guided practice and review.

## step-3-heading
Continue with coaching

## step-3-body
Follow-up sessions help teachers apply what they have learned, troubleshoot problems, and refine their materials. Coaching is arranged around the goals agreed with school leadership.

## services-heading
Training and coaching

## services
- **Half-day and full-day workshops.** Hands-on training for a whole staff or department. Teachers work with the school's selected AI tools and rework a lesson or document of their own.
- **On-site coaching.** Individual and team support using current curriculum, materials, and instructional questions. Sessions focus on teachers' preparation for their own classes.
- **Virtual sessions and office hours.** Live training and continuing support between school visits.
- **Keynotes and briefings.** Presentations for staff, leadership teams, and school boards. The school receives the presentation materials.
- **Family information nights.** An evening discussion of student AI use, school expectations, privacy, and families' questions.

## options-heading
Choose the scope that fits

## options
- **Alcuin Start** — A first engagement for a single school: initial training plus follow-up coaching.
- **The Alcuin Year** — Training and continuing support across a full school year.
- **Alcuin District** — The same work delivered across a district of three or more campuses.
- **Summer Institute** — Concentrated, multi-day training in the weeks before the school year begins.

## options-note
Alcuin Start and The Alcuin Year have elementary and secondary versions. Student-facing work is adapted to the age group.

## pricing-line
Pricing is by proposal, based on the scope, school or district size, and services included.

## prep-heading
What to bring

## prep-body
Bring lessons, unit plans, rubrics, assessments, and other planning materials. We use the AI platform your school has selected and can help leaders evaluate options if that decision is still open.

Student work, grades, and records stay out of training sessions. Exercises that call for student data use invented examples or aggregate information that does not identify students.
