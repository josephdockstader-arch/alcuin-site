## values-heading

## values-intro
Seven, sequenced from the handshake to the child.

## values
- **Integrity** — We do what we say, by when we say, at the standard we say — and when we miss, we say so first.
- **Privacy** — We protect the privacy of students and teachers absolutely, even when protecting it costs us the contract.
- **Mastery** — We pursue mastery the way we ask teachers to: practicing, measuring, and improving our own craft continuously.
- **Critical Thinking** — We develop critical thinkers: every solution must strengthen, never shortcut, the thinking of teachers and students.
- **Core Solutions** — We work by cause and effect, never by crisis — if it doesn't reach the core problem, we don't call it a solution.
- **Empathy** — We listen before we prescribe: every engagement starts with the lived reality of teachers and students.
- **Teacher Centric** — We build for the teacher first, because every child's learning flows through them.

Meet the founder

## background-body
Joseph taught ELA and humanities for fifteen years across middle school, high school, and college, designing curriculum and helping students become more capable readers, writers, and thinkers.

Rigorous professional development in critical thinking has been central to his career, particularly Paul and Elder's critical thinking framework.

He now helps educators configure AI tools, build reusable workflows, and create instructional materials, including course websites. His training brings the same attention to the thinking students are being asked to do.

## approach-heading
Keep the learning goal in view

## approach-body
AI can help teachers prepare materials and help students access an assignment. Deciding where it belongs starts with the skill the student is meant to develop. Assistance should make that learning possible without completing it for the student.
